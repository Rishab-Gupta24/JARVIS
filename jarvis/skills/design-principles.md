Visual design basics:
- Hierarchy: one thing is most important on each screen (the fighters, the hero headline); make it biggest and
  highest-contrast. Secondary info (timer, score) smaller; decoration quietest.
- Spacing on a scale (4, 8, 16, 24, 32, 48 px); align things to a grid; consistent margins (at least 16 px from edges).
- Colour 60-30-10: 60% background/neutral, 30% secondary, 10% accent for what must be noticed. Use the PALETTE names.
- Type: at most two fonts/sizes families; body text 16 px+ on the web, HUD numbers large and bold in games.
- Shapes speak: rounded = friendly, sharp = aggressive; keep one style across the whole project.
- Placeholder looks (plain grey boxes, default fonts, everything the same size) are bugs, not style.
