Colour and contrast:
- Every colour comes from PALETTE in the config module; no new hex values in scenes or CSS rules.
- Contrast (WCAG): text 4.5:1 against what's behind it, large text and important shapes 3:1. Characters must stand out
  from the background at a glance; the studio already corrected the palette for this, keep it.
- Don't signal with colour alone: also shape, position or a label (colour-blind players).
- Dark backgrounds: use slightly desaturated light colours for text, not pure white everywhere; keep the brightest
  colour for the accent.
- State colours: hit/danger = the palette's danger colour, success = its positive colour, consistent everywhere.
